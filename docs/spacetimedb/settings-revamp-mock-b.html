Settings revamp — B: Settings sheet, saves as you go

B moves app settings out of the modal and into the frame's right sheet, the same place where creating and editing already happen. The app stays visible and reacts to each change right away. There's no Save button: each control saves on its own with a revision check and shows a small "Saved" status. Invalid text never saves, and a toast offers Undo. The Dar settings page gets an overview where every section is a card showing its current value, and the section opens in the right sheet. Prayer Times leads with a day timeline and a countdown, and settings changes mark the times they affect in the week behind the sheet.

  • Settings is a sheet, not a modal. The gear opens a 400px right sheet with underlined tabs (General, Import and export, Shortcuts). It replaces the details pane, and Escape or the gear closes it. On phones it's a bottom sheet.
  • Saves as you go. Switches, segmented controls and selects save when changed. Text and number fields save on blur or Enter, and only when valid. Each row shows Saving…, then Saved. Each save checks the revision, and a conflict shows inline with Reload.
  • Undo instead of Discard. The last change shows a toast with Undo. Unsaved-change prompts are only needed for invalid fields, which keep their error and are never saved. This changes the current "explicit discard" contract.
  • Settings overview. Your profile header and one card per section with its current value (theme, timezone, number of passwords or apps). Cards open in the right sheet, and Appearance can be changed straight from its card.
  • Prayer Times. A today band with a timeline of every prayer, a "now" marker and a large countdown. Below it, a compact week where each day row lists all its times. When settings change, the affected times are marked with a dot.
  • Same rows everywhere. Label and short explanation on the left, control on the right, and a 16px gap between sections, in app sheets, Dar settings and on phones.

B3 · Prayer Times — dark, today timeline and compact week

Today is a band at the top of the sheet: the next prayer in large type, a countdown, and a timeline from Fajr to Isha with a "now" marker. Past prayers are filled, the next one glows, and Sunrise is a dashed solar marker. The week below has the same columns in every row, so you can compare days at a glance. Opening a day still shows the daily timetable in the right pane, with Back to week on phones.
