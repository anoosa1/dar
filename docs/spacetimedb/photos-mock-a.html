Photos — A: timeline library with immersive viewer

Direction A brings Immich's library model into Dar's frame. The sidebar holds views and the library; the middle sheet is a justified timeline grouped by day with memories on top and a year/month scrubber on its edge; a photo opens an immersive viewer that covers the workspace with the info panel on the right. Explore, Map, albums and sharing reuse the right sheet like every other Dar app.

  • Library: timeline by day/month/year with scrubber, memories, favorites, archive, trash (30 days), locked folder (PIN), duplicates review, stacks (burst, RAW+JPEG), Live Photos and video.
  • Organize: albums (shared and personal, cover, description, sort), tags, descriptions, ratings, edit date/time and location, bulk actions with Undo.
  • People: on-device face detection and clustering on the server, naming, merge, hide, birth dates, link to a Contact.
  • Search: smart (CLIP) search by description, by person, place, camera, date, file type and text in photos (OCR); results in the shared search.
  • Places: reverse geocoding (offline city database) and a clustered map with filters.
  • Viewer: zoom, slideshow, EXIF info, rotate/crop/filters as non-destructive edits, video streaming (transcoded), download original or edited.
  • Sharing: albums with Dar users (view or contribute), partner sharing, shared links with expiry, password, upload and download switches.
  • Immich: connect an Immich server (import or two-way), and/or let the Immich mobile app back up to Dar. Uploads are deduplicated by checksum.
  • Platform: photos are immutable blobs in Files-backed storage, thumbnails and previews generated in the background, backups include Photos, per-account privacy.

A1 · Timeline — light

Memories ("On this day", trips, people) lead the timeline. Days show the place and a select-all circle; tiles keep their aspect ratio. The scrubber jumps by year and month and shows the date while dragging. Days/Months/Years changes density.
