Discord in Chat — servers and voice mocks

Discord joins Chat as a second account tab, signed in with your own Discord account through Discord's QR login (the mobile app approves it; Dar never sees the password). The Discord tab has its own structure — direct messages, then servers with their text and voice channels — and reads like Discord: flat message rows with replies, reactions, link previews and threads. Voice runs in this browser: your microphone, Discord's mandatory end-to-end encryption (DAVE) in the bridge, and everyone else's audio mixed here. Two directions differ in where an active call lives; the server tree, text channels, sign-in, phones and states are shared.

  • A · Voice stage: opening or joining a voice channel shows it in the main sheet — a grid of participants with a speaking ring, the encryption badge, and a control bar (mute, deafen, voice settings, leave). The voice channel's own chat sits in the right sheet.
  • B · Voice dock: a call keeps running while you read and write in text channels. The dock at the bottom of the sidebar shows the channel and the controls; participants appear under the voice channel in the tree, as in Discord, and the dock opens a list with per-person volume and the call code.
  • Shared: the account tab switches WhatsApp and Discord apart; servers collapse; unread channels are bold with counts; muted channels are dimmed. Members list on the right in text channels. Deafen implies mute; leaving is red and last.
  • Sign-in and risk: Link Discord shows Discord's QR code with a plain notice that Discord does not allow other apps on personal accounts and can limit or close the account.
  • Voice states: microphone blocked (listen only), reconnecting, an empty channel with Join muted, and the call code to compare with others.
  • Not in scope yet: video and screen share, stage channels, server settings and moderation, creating servers. Text in Discord and voice share the Chat composer and menus.
