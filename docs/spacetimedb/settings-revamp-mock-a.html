Settings revamp — A: Settings hub

A keeps today's model and makes it calmer. App settings stay a centered modal, but it gets bigger, with vertical sections on the left and settings shown as rows grouped into cards. Each row puts the label and a one-line explanation on the left and the control on the right. Nothing saves until you press Save. A save bar at the bottom counts unsaved changes, and a small accent mark shows which rows changed. The Dar settings page uses the same rows and cards, and editing happens in the right pane. In Prayer Times, the week becomes a timetable grid, and its settings get a live preview of today's times.

  • One modal for every app. 820×600 on desktop with sections on the left: General, Import and export, Keyboard shortcuts. App icon and "Saved for your account" in the header.
  • Rows, not stacked fields. A label plus a short explanation on the left, the control on the right. Two or three choices use a segmented control, on/off uses a switch, and longer lists use the shared select.
  • Explicit save stays. The save bar shows "N unsaved changes" with Discard and Save. Closing while dirty uses the shared discard confirmation. After saving, the bar reads "All changes saved".
  • Cross-links, not duplicates. Calendar shows the account timezone read-only with a link to Regional settings instead of a second timezone control.
  • Dar settings page. The same cards and rows, grouped under Personal and Administration. Creating and editing (app passwords, OIDC clients, people) opens in the right pane.
  • Prayer Times. The week becomes a 7×6 timetable with today and the next prayer highlighted. The day pane gets a countdown card. Settings get a preview column showing the new times before you save.

A1 · Calendar settings — light, one unsaved change

The modal sits over the frame. Week begins was changed from Monday to Sunday, so it shows the accent mark and the bar counts it. The timezone row is read-only and links to Regional settings, where it is actually edited.

A4 · Prayer Times settings — dark, editing with a live preview

This is the same modal with a third column. The preview recalculates today's times from the unsaved form, and changed times show the old value struck through, so the effect of a method or offset is clear before saving. The method is a radio list showing its Fajr/Isha angles, Asr is a two-choice segmented control, and offsets are steppers.
