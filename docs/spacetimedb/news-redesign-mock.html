Why io_uring batch submission cuts tail latency in half

Nadia Mansour · Today, 9:49 PM · 4 min read

Local-first personal clouds spend most of their request budget reading small immutable chunks off NVMe drives.

P99 read latency across 16 concurrent gallery requests.

Batching without unbounded queues

By packing read requests into a single submission and capping in-flight rings, throughput rises while memory stays flat.
