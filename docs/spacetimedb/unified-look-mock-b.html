B — Agent rows and composer, inside an app rail with floating sheets

This keeps the Agent's rows, composer, context cards, drawer and focused panes, and adds two shared structures. A persistent 72px app rail on the far left switches apps in one click and holds settings and the account. A shared top bar keeps the centered cross-app search that was selected earlier. Content sits in rounded floating sheets on the soft background. Selection uses a tonal violet pill (Material 3 navigation style). Every app's primary create action is an extended button at the same place, the top of the sidebar. The Agent rejoins this shared frame instead of using its own chrome.

  • App rail 72px: Dar mark, apps as 52×30 icon pills with labels, active is tonal, badges for due items, settings and avatar at bottom.
  • Top bar 56px: app title aligned over the sidebar, a centered 600px search pill, and app-level icons on the right.
  • Sheets: center and right panes are 16px-radius bordered surfaces with an 8px gutter on the soft background.
